## Ship decision

The exact merged source tree **may be promoted to production** because:

* merged CI and the complete local suite passed;
* the identical source tree is healthy on staging;
* Convex is already deployed and compatible;
* transport and review persistence were demonstrated live;
* rollback to the exact prior production image exists; and
* production defaults remain `baseline` / `control` / `low`.

The promotion must not set `instant-v1`, the candidate model, or minimal reasoning. It also must not be described as proving Malaysian voice quality, the returning-visitor target, audible-latency gates, false-endpoint safety, contact-correction parity, or completion of the full instant-voice roadmap. Owner authorization permits deployment; it is not evidence for those claims.
